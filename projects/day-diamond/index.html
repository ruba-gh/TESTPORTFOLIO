Overview

Day Diamond starts from a simple observation: free time can disappear into scrolling because it is the easiest option. The concept makes intentional use of small free moments feel lighter and more rewarding through short real-world challenges, points and leaderboard motivation. The name reflects the idea of finding something valuable inside an ordinary day.

View Figma prototype ↗

Design decisions

Real-world firstThe core loop points users toward short activities away from passive scrolling.
Small commitmentChallenges are framed around free moments rather than demanding a complete lifestyle overhaul.
Visible motivationPoints and leaderboard mechanics provide a lightweight layer of progress around real-world actions.
Playful framingThe product treats reduced screen time as something approachable and rewarding rather than punitive.
