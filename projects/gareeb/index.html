PROJECT 02 · COMMUNITY DELIVERY APP

Gareeb

A local delivery platform that connects people who need small items with nearby users already going to the same place.

Role
UX/UI Design
Tools
Figma
Documented deliverables
User Flow · Service Concept · UI Screens · Prototype

Overview

The context

Gareeb was created around a common daily problem: needing one or two small items without wanting to make a full trip or pay a high delivery fee. The concept turns everyday neighborhood movement into shared opportunities, letting users request quick pickups from nearby places such as pharmacies, bakeries, groceries and local shops.

View Figma prototype ↗

Design Approach

What the experience focuses on

This case study only includes design decisions that are supported by the documented project scope and available interface material.

Nearby activityThe home experience surfaces active trips around the user rather than beginning with a store catalogue.
Request clarityA request captures the item, quantity, notes and trip context without turning a small errand into a long order flow.
Shared trip modelThe service concept is built around people who are already heading to the requested place.
Status visibilityThe order experience makes progress from acceptance to delivery visible in one place.
