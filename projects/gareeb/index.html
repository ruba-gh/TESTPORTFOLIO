Project background

A local delivery platform that connects people who need small items with nearby users already going to the same place.

Gareeb was created to solve a common daily problem: needing one or two small items without wanting to make a full trip or pay high delivery fees. The app turns everyday neighborhood movement into shared opportunities, allowing users to request quick pickups from nearby stores such as pharmacies, bakeries, groceries, and local shops.
