Project background

A smart budgeting app designed to make expense tracking easier and support healthier spending habits.

Budgie was created to support adults in Saudi Arabia who want better financial stability but struggle with consistency, impulsive spending, and manual budgeting. The app reduces the effort of tracking expenses by automatically recording and categorizing spending, giving users clear insights, budget alerts, and real-time guidance before they pay.

Bring spending
together.

Import transactions with SMS and Shortcuts, bringing everyday purchases into one budgeting experience. Less manual input means more room to stay aware of your money.

See the patterns
behind the purchases.

Categories and rules organize transactions into a clearer picture of everyday spending. Insights help connect individual purchases with the bigger picture.

Clarity before
complexity.

Budgie’s onboarding was simplified from six steps to three. A lock-screen widget keeps useful information close, bringing the experience into everyday moments.
