Budgie

A smart budgeting app designed to make expense tracking easier and support healthier spending habits.

Overview

Budgie was created to support adults in Saudi Arabia who want better financial stability but struggle with consistency, impulsive spending and manual budgeting. The app reduces the effort of tracking expenses by automatically recording and categorizing spending, then gives users clear insights, budget alerts and real-time guidance before they pay.

View Figma prototype ↗

Design decisions

Lower tracking effortAutomatic recording and categorization reduce the work involved in keeping a budget current.
Before-spend guidanceThe experience is designed to support a decision while it is happening, not only report spending afterward.
Visible budget stateBudget alerts and clear summaries keep the user aware of where their money is going.
Quick accessWidget support makes a budget check available without requiring a full app session.
