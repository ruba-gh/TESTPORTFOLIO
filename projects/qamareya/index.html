Qamareya

A smart women’s health app designed to support different health stages through tracking, education and specialist consultations.

Overview

The context

Qamareya brings pregnancy tracking, fertility tracking and menstrual cycle tracking into one unified platform. Its name is inspired by the Arabic word “Qamar” — moon — reflecting rhythm, phases and natural cycles. The experience is structured to help women follow health changes, access educational content and communicate with specialists when needed.

Design Approach

What the experience focuses on

This case study only includes design decisions that are supported by the documented project scope and available interface material.

One information structureCycle, fertility and pregnancy tracking are organized within one product rather than fragmented across separate experiences.
Calm hierarchyThe visual direction supports information-heavy health tracking without making every state compete for attention.
Stage-aware flowThe information architecture accounts for different health stages while keeping the experience coherent.
System consistencyA design system was part of the documented deliverables, supporting repeatable interface patterns across the product.
